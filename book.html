MY BOOK

Retracing My Steps

By Alvin Dube

A personal journey through hardship, money, loss, mistakes, resilience, and self-discovery.

It is written to encourage people who may feel stuck, discouraged or afraid of taking the next step in life. Sometimes our circumstances are difficult, but they do not have to determine the person we become.

It is a reminder that no matter where life takes you, you can always begin retracing your steps.
