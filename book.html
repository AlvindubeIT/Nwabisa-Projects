MY BOOK

It Is Better to Face This World Strong Than Weak

By Alvin Dube

This book is a collection of real-life experiences, lessons and reflections about facing difficult situations, making decisions and becoming stronger through life's challenges.

It is written to encourage people who may feel stuck, discouraged or afraid of taking the next step in life. Sometimes our circumstances are difficult, but they do not have to determine the person we become.
